## 📝 1. Official 240-Word Project Description (For NASA Submission Portal)

> Change is always occurring in the Earth’s interconnected environmental system. However, a single Earth science process can drive opposite trends in different regions—such as monsoon rain compressing into destructive mountain cloudbursts on India's Western Ghats while simultaneously causing prolonged 15–20 day drying deficits across the Indo-Gangetic agrarian breadbasket. The multitude of Earth science variables makes it difficult to isolate structural trends from temporary 1-year weather noise.
> 
> The **India Earth System Trend Detective** is an AI-powered forensic climate intelligence platform engineered to separate genuine permanent decadal shifts from natural weather fluctuations across 31 continuous years (1995–2025). Trained strictly on authenticated NASA Earth observation streams—**NASA GPM (IMERG 0.1° Precipitation Radar)**, **NASA MODIS (Terra/Aqua LST >45°C)**, and **NASA SMAP (0–100 cm Root-Zone Soil Radar)**—calibrated against the **India Meteorological Department (IMD 1991–2020) 30-year normal climatology**.
> 
> Featuring a dual **WebGL Three.js 3D System Architecture** and an interactive **Leaflet Map Dashboard (zoomable down to campus/home level 21x)**, the platform answers NASA’s four foundational forensic questions for every Indian agro-climatic zone: *What is changing, Where is it changing, How much is it changing, and Is it scientifically significant (>99% confidence via Mann-Kendall and FDR q < 0.05)?*
> 
> The system delivers a 30-year live time-lapse player, a compound climate risk analyzer, and instant executive client reports exportable to Microsoft Word (`.doc`), Print/PDF, and CSV.
